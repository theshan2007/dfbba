About DFBBA

Sri Lanka's Premier Bartender & Barista Academy

Established in Dambulla, DFBBA is an officially approved TVEC training center dedicated to producing world-class hospitality professionals. We combine rigorous theory with intensive hands-on lab sessions — preparing students for careers in top hotels, resorts, and establishments locally and internationally.

TVEC Registered Center TVEC Approved Certificates Practical Bar & Barista Labs Hotel Placement Support

Fully Equipped Training Labs — Dedicated barista bars, cocktail stations, and professional-grade equipment used by real 5-star hotels.

Small Batch Learning — Maximum 12–15 students per batch to ensure personalized, intensive instruction from day one.

Industry Placement Network — Direct partnerships with 50+ hotels, resorts and restaurants for guaranteed internship and employment referrals.

Flexible Scheduling — Morning, evening, and weekend batches available to suit working professionals and school leavers alike.
